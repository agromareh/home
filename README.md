## The Vision

Agriculture feeds billions and anchors entire economies, yet most crop-health decisions in the field are still made with delayed information, fragmented records, and reactive interventions.

**AgroMareh** is closing that gap by turning everyday field observations into a compounding, AI-driven intelligence network. Starting in Nigeria and built to scale across Africa and global agricultural markets, we are creating the operating layer where **computer vision, field agronomy, and agro-supply connectivity** converge.

---

## What We Do

AgroMareh transforms a single crop image into a closed-loop intelligence workflow designed for real-world field conditions:

1. **Detect** — AI-assisted computer vision analyzes crop images across an expanding range of crops and conditions.
2. **Recommend** — Translates each diagnosis and confidence assessment into clear, actionable crop-health guidance.
3. **Report** — Voice-enabled reporting makes field recommendations accessible, fast to act on, and easy to share across teams.
4. **Record** — Captures structured diagnostic history over time, replacing scattered notes with a living operational record.
5. **Learn** — Every field observation strengthens validation, institutional insight, and long-term agricultural intelligence.
