AI Decision-Support Notice

AgroMareh's crop-health assessments, confidence scores, voice-enabled reports, and management recommendations are generated using artificial intelligence and computer vision. They are provided strictly for informational and decision-support purposes and do not replace professional agronomic inspection, laboratory testing, or regulatory compliance.

1. AI-Assisted Diagnostics & Limitations

Computer-vision models analyze visual patterns in submitted photographs. Accuracy can be affected by lighting, camera focus, crop variety, growth stage, overlapping pests/diseases, nutrient deficiencies, soil conditions, or local environmental factors.

  • Any reported benchmark (such as ~90% detection accuracy observed in internal and Middle Belt field testing) is a historical testing metric, not a warranty of performance on every image or farm;
  • AI outputs may occasionally produce false positives, false negatives, or incomplete recommendations;
  • Human-in-the-loop review by a trained agronomist or extension specialist is strongly recommended before making high-impact field decisions.

2. Agrochemical, Phytosanitary & Safety Responsibility

Before applying any pesticide, fungicide, herbicide, fertilizer, or biological input, the farm operator or institution is solely responsible for:

  • Verifying that the product is legally registered and approved for the target crop in their jurisdiction (e.g., NAFDAC/regulatory authorities in Nigeria or equivalent national agencies worldwide);
  • Following official manufacturer labels, dosage rates, personal protective equipment (PPE) requirements, and pre-harvest intervals;
  • Complying with local environmental, water-protection, and food-safety regulations.

3. Independent Agro Suppliers (supplier.agromareh.org)

AgroMareh connects agricultural buyers and institutions with independent third-party agro suppliers. Unless AgroMareh expressly signs a direct supply contract:

  • AgroMareh does not manufacture, store, inspect, ship, or warrant third-party agricultural inputs;
  • Each agro supplier is solely responsible for product authenticity, regulatory licensing, quality, pricing, delivery, and after-sales warranty;
  • Buyers should perform standard commercial due diligence before completing high-value transactions.

4. Research & Institutional Benchmarks

References to field testing, institutional collaborations (including FUEP), or intellectual property (including our Registered Nigerian Utility Model Patent) describe AgroMareh's ongoing R&D and commercialization pathway and do not imply regulatory certification or government endorsement of any specific crop treatment.
