Our Privacy Commitment

AGRO MAREH TECH ("AgroMareh") respects your privacy and designs its agricultural intelligence platforms to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and comparable international privacy laws. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Data Controller & Contact

AGRO MAREH TECH is the data controller for personal data collected through home.agromareh.org, agromareh.org, app.agromareh.org, supplier.agromareh.org, admin.agromareh.org, and the AgroMareh Android application. For institutional enterprise deployments, AgroMareh may act as a data processor under a signed Data Processing Addendum (DPA).

Privacy & Data Protection Contact: contactus@agromareh.org | +234 814 123 2871.

3. Lawful Bases for Processing

Depending on your jurisdiction and how you interact with AgroMareh, we process personal data under one or more of the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: To provide the AgroMareh app, diagnostic records, and supplier portal you requested;
  • Consent: Where you opt in to camera, microphone (voice reporting), or location permissions on your device, or submit a voluntary enquiry;
  • Legitimate Interests: Securing our infrastructure, preventing fraud, communicating with institutional contacts, and improving diagnostic models using de-identified/aggregated data where this does not override your rights;
  • Legal Obligation: Complying with applicable laws, tax/regulatory requirements, or lawful court orders.

4. How We Share Data & Subprocessors

We do not sell or rent personal data to data brokers or third-party advertisers. We share data only with:

  • Core Infrastructure Providers: Cloud hosting, database, and edge security providers (including Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Cloudflare) bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations;
  • Your Institution: If your account is provisioned by a university, research institute, or enterprise farm, authorized administrators within your organization may access organizational diagnostic records;
  • Agro-Supply Connectivity: Where you explicitly request to connect with or register as an agro supplier;
  • Legal & Safety Authorities: Only where required by valid legal process or to protect vital safety and platform security.

5. International Data Transfers

Because AgroMareh operates on global cloud infrastructure (such as AWS and Cloudflare), data may be processed outside your country of residence. Whenever personal data is transferred internationally (including from Nigeria, the EEA, or the UK), we implement appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), adequacy mechanisms, and encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, honor institutional agreements, or comply with legal obligations. De-identified and aggregated agronomic datasets that cannot identify an individual may be retained for ongoing agricultural research and model accuracy improvements.

We apply technical and organizational safeguards including HTTPS/TLS encryption, access controls (including Cloudflare Zero Trust for administrative interfaces), least-privilege infrastructure policies, and regular monitoring.

7. Your Global Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (including under Nigeria's NDPA 2023, EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, Canada's PIPEDA, and Brazil's LGPD), you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • Erase ("Right to be Forgotten") your personal data, subject to lawful retention exceptions;
  • Restrict or Object to certain processing activities;
  • Data Portability — receive your provided data in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • Withdraw Consent at any time (for example, revoking camera/microphone permissions in your Android settings);
  • Non-Discrimination — you will never be penalized for exercising your privacy rights;
  • Lodge a Complaint with your local supervisory authority (such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), an EU Data Protection Authority, or the UK ICO).

To exercise any right, email contactus@agromareh.org with the subject line Privacy Request. We will verify your identity and respond within statutory timeframes (typically within 30 days).

8. Children's Privacy

AgroMareh is designed for institutional, research, enterprise, and adult agricultural users and is not directed to children under 16 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data, contact contactus@agromareh.org for prompt deletion.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features, infrastructure, or legal requirements. Updates take effect when posted here with a revised "Last Updated" date.
