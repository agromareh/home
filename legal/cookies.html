Privacy-First Design

home.agromareh.org does not use third-party advertising cookies, behavioral profiling pixels, or cross-site trackers. We only use strictly necessary security/session mechanisms and a single local browser preference for Light/Dark mode.

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser when you visit a website. Local Storage is a browser-side key-value store that remains on your device and is not automatically transmitted on every HTTP request.

3. Consent & Opt-Out

Under EU/UK ePrivacy rules, GDPR, and Nigeria's NDPA 2023, strictly necessary cookies and user-requested UI preferences do not require advertising-style tracking consent. If AgroMareh ever introduces optional analytics or marketing cookies in the future, we will update this policy and request your explicit opt-in consent before setting them.

4. Managing Browser Storage

You can clear or block cookies and Local Storage at any time through your browser settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent sign-in on app.agromareh.org, supplier.agromareh.org, or admin.agromareh.org.
