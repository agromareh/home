To protect farmers, research institutions, agro suppliers, and our cloud infrastructure, you agree not to use any AgroMareh platform to:

3. Responsible Security Disclosure

If you discover a security vulnerability in any AgroMareh website, Android app, API, or portal, please report it privately to contactus@agromareh.org with the subject line Security Disclosure. Please do not access other users' data, degrade service availability, or publicly disclose the issue before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate it.
